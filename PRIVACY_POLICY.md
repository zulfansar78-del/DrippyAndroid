Thank you for using Drippy. This privacy policy explains what data Drippy handles and how.

## Short version

**Drippy collects nothing. Drippy sends nothing.** All your reminders, logs, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device. No account is required. No data leaves your phone.

## What data Drippy stores on your device

Drippy stores the following information locally in your device's app storage:

- The reminders you create (name, time, type, custom message)
- Your daily progress log (cups of water, meals, vitamins, diet check-ins)
- Your streak count and the date of your last successful streak day
- Your achievement progress
- Your settings (water goal, snooze duration, theme, sound preferences, pause state)

This data is sandboxed by Android and is only accessible to the Drippy app on your specific device. It is not synced to any server, account, or cloud.

## What data Drippy collects from you

**None.** Drippy does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or analyze any personal data. There is no analytics tracking, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting service, and no remote logging.

## Permissions Drippy requests, and why

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** — to show reminders on your screen.
- **Exact alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` / `USE_EXACT_ALARM`)** — to fire reminders at the precise times you've set, even if the screen is off.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`)** — so reminders can vibrate the phone.
- **Receive boot completed (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`)** — to re-schedule your reminders if your phone restarts, so they don't disappear after a reboot.
- **Wake lock (`WAKE_LOCK`)** — to wake the phone briefly to fire a scheduled reminder.

None of these permissions grant access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, files, or any personal information.

## Children's privacy

Drippy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is safe for general audiences.

## Data deletion

To delete all data Drippy has stored, you can:
- Open Drippy → tap **⚙️ Settings** → **🗑️ Reset all data**, OR
- Uninstall the app from your device — Android will remove all of Drippy's local storage.

There is no server-side data to request deletion of, because no data ever leaves your device.
